Youth Pitch Scholarship

Read The Art of Culture, pick an idea, lesson or story from it, and pitch a business built on it. Show us how culture, heritage and community can become something you build.

Prize$1,000
Ages13–19
Open toUS Students
DeadlineJan 15, 2027

Who Can Enter

Students ages 13 to 19 who live in the United States. Entrants under 18 need approval from a parent or guardian.

One winner receives the $1,000 scholarship.

What to SubmitWritten pitch · 500–800 words

  1. The book connection. Which part of the book inspired your idea, and why.
  2. The problem. What need or gap your business fills.
  3. The business. Your product or service, who it serves, and how it makes money.
  4. The culture. How your business honors or shares culture.
  5. The first step. What you would do with $1,000 to get started.
+20

  • Share it as an unlisted YouTube or Google Drive link
  • The book must be visible in your video, or it earns no extra credit
